The raw common multi-feature risk sum R is evaluated at each successful normal candidate with valid separated geometry. Each point is a within-trial mean (left) or within-trial sample SD (right); large symbols and bars are the across-trial mean and sample SD of those statistics. All observed trials are included, including pre-termination segments of failed trials. R may exceed one and is not an empirical collision probability. Extreme saved planning-covariance states remain included; robust P05 chance-budget margin summaries in the main figure and the outlier audit complement these moment statistics. The two levels of SD describe temporal dispersion within a trial and dispersion between trials, respectively; neither is a confidence interval.
