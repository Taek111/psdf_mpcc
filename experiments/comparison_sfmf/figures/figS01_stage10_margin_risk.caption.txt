Stage 10 (1 s) diagnostics for all 120 trials, including observations before failure. Small symbols: one trial statistic; connected large symbols: arithmetic mean across ten trials per method/width; bars: sample SD (ddof=1), not confidence intervals. Within each trial, successful normal solves are evaluated using freshly recomputed geometry and their saved planning covariance. (a) P05 of phi-d_col, d_col=0.05 mm. (b) Fraction with phi-d_col < -1 micrometre. (c) Fraction with R>0.1+1e-6, conditional on phi>d_min=0.1 mm and a valid separated feature model. (d) P05 of the common chance-budget residual epsilon-R, epsilon=0.1 and R=sum_i p_i; zero is the constraint boundary. R is a common local Gaussian/Boole surrogate evaluated on both controllers, not a measured collision probability, and may exceed one. Domain exits are excluded only from (c,d) and are reported separately in the coverage figure/tables. Failure-trial observation lengths differ; see paired common-time sensitivity. Stage 10 is a predicted horizon state, not an executed next pose.
