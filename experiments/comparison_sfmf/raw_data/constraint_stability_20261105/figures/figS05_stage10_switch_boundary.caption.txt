Stage-10 switching-boundary sensitivity across all widths and both source controllers, without selecting a route segment or successful trials. Two endpoints lie 10 micrometres on each side of each localized canonical-feature boundary in the body-motion metric ||delta_xy||+r|delta_theta|. The original left-cycle body-frame planning covariance is fixed for both endpoints and both evaluator functions. No extrapolation past the observed nominal segment is used. Both endpoints must be separated, valid and MF-active, with ordinary inverse-CDF branches; clipping and linear-extension events are excluded from this primary figure. Each small symbol is one trial mean absolute finite-offset change; large symbols and bars are the mean and sample SD across trials with eligible events. n is the number of such trials, not independent boundary events. Top: standardized SF and MF residual changes in common quantile units. Bottom: changes of epsilon-p_selected and epsilon-R in percentage points, using the same event set. R is a local Boole surrogate sum, not measured collision probability. Five and fifty micrometre offsets, all active branches, P95/RMS, and all excluded-event counts are retained in the tables. These are local finite-offset sensitivities, not mathematical discontinuity limits or proof of improved closed-loop completion.
